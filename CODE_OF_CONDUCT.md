# Community conduct

MicroDuckling welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of contributing. Help make it a place where someone can ask a basic question, report a failed print or disagree about a design without being belittled.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and direct. Critique the design, code or claim; do not attack the person.
- Explain your reasoning and evidence. Allow for different tools, budgets, abilities and access to hardware.
- Ask before assuming intent. Accept corrections and give others room to learn.
- Credit other people's work and respect their time, privacy and boundaries.
- Keep discussion relevant and constructive. Repeated unwanted contact, insults, harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

These expectations apply in this repository's issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces, and when someone is explicitly representing the project elsewhere.

## Raising a concern

Use GitHub's available reporting tools for abusive content. To contact the maintainer, consult [itchson's GitHub profile](https://github.com/itchson) for any available contact method. Keep sensitive details and other people's private information out of public issues.

Describe the behavior and provide relevant links or context when it is appropriate to share them. You do not need to debate the person involved before reporting a concern.

## Moderation

The maintainer may ask participants to change their behavior, edit or remove content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation. The response should reflect the behavior, its impact and whether it is repeated. Serious abuse can result in an immediate restriction.

Moderation should be explained where practical without exposing private information. A participant can ask the maintainer to reconsider a decision and provide additional context through an available contact method. There is currently no separate moderation or appeals team.

These are original guidelines for this project and can be improved through a pull request.
